# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

This policy covers the website lollipopkit.com and the apps published by lollipopkit. An app that has a privacy policy of its own, listed under [App privacy policies](#app-privacy-policies), is described there in full; that policy takes precedence for the app.

## This website

- lollipopkit.com is a static site. The site itself sets no cookies and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.
- The language you choose is stored in your browser's local storage and never sent anywhere.
- The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes request data such as your IP address and browser user agent to deliver and protect the site, as described in [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Google user data

The following lollipopkit apps access Google user data through Google APIs:

- **MFuse** (macOS) requests `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive` to mount your Google Drive in Finder, and reads your Google account name and email address to label the connection. All of this data is processed only on your Mac. Details: [MFuse privacy policy](https://mfuse.lollipopkit.com/privacy.html).

For every app listed above, lollipopkit:

- uses Google user data only to provide the features the user requests in that app;
- does not transfer Google user data to lollipopkit or to any third party;
- does not use Google user data for advertising, and does not sell it;
- does not allow any person to read Google user data;
- does not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs by these apps adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. No other lollipopkit app accesses Google user data; an app that does in the future will be listed here before it requests access.

You can revoke an app's access to your Google account at any time at [myaccount.google.com/connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
